# Glossary

Words and their meanings to help you understand the documentation more easily!

</div>

---

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Regolith** | Martian soil and loose rocky dust covering the planet's surface. | The first filter stage traps coarse particles of Martian regolith. |
| **Perchlorate** $\text{ClO}_4^-$ | Toxic chemical salts found naturally in the regolith. | Special filters are required to remove dangerous perchlorates from the water. |
| **Bioremediation** | Using living organisms, like bacteria or plants, to clean up contamination. | Bioremediation helps break down organic waste compounds safely. |
| **Pyrolysis** | Heating organic material without oxygen to break it down thermally without burning. | Pyrolysis converts organic waste into valuable carbon material. |
| **Biochar** | A stable, carbon-rich charcoal produced through the pyrolysis process. | Biochar can be used as an extra filtration medium in the system. |
| **Reverse Osmosis** | The high-pressure purification process used by the main water machine. | Reverse osmosis removes microscopic dissolved impurities from the water. |
