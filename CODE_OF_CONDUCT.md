# Code of Conduct

</div>

## My Pledge
I am committed to keeping **Regolith Water Loop** an open, welcoming, and safe space for anyone who wants to learn, review, or contribute, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Standards
* Keep discussions constructive, evidence-based, and focused on the work.
* Be respectful and professional when giving or receiving feedback.
* Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, and trolling will not be tolerated.
* Do not publish private information about others without explicit consent.

## Enforcement
As the sole maintainer, I reserve the right to edit or delete any comments, issues, or pull requests that violate these standards, and to block anyone who repeatedly disrupts the project.
